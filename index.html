    Check 1: Form errors

    Check 2: Changing page

    Choose Services in the main menu.

    Were you told that the page had changed?

    Check 3: Theme control

    Find the theme control at the top of the page.

    Could you tell which theme is active?

    Check 2: Footer

    Move into the footer at the bottom of the homepage.

    Could you see each link and the sign-up form as you reached it?

    Check 3: Skipping the menu

    Start at the top of a page.

    Could you get to the main content without going through every menu link?

    Check 2: Zoom

    Zoom to 200%, then 400%.

    Could you read everything without scrolling sideways?

    Check 3: Themes

    Try the Light, Coral and Dark themes.

    Was all the text and every control easy to see?

    Check 2: Voice commands

    Use your usual commands to open a link, and to press Send on the contact form.

    Did the website respond?

    Check 3: Social media links

    Select each social media link in the homepage footer.

    Could you select each one without hitting the wrong link?

    Check 2: Video captions

    Play the video at the top of the homepage.

    If it has speech, were captions available, and could you follow it?

    Check 3: Stopping the video

    Could you stop the video once it had started?

    Check 2: Movement

    Look at the homepage.

    Did anything moving on the homepage distract you, and could you stop it? Yes means nothing distracted you, or you could stop it.

    Check 3: Social media links on a phone

    On a phone, tap each social media link in the homepage footer.

    Could you tap each one without hitting the wrong link?
